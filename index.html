What we asked, and why

How do literal, mixed and ironic evaluations intersect with gender stereotypes and accounts of harassment, and do reviews that describe toxic communities recommend games less?

Where the data comes from

Source
KieranPoc’s Kaggle Steam reviews dataset
Provenance
Collected via the Steam reviews API
Unit of analysis
One Steam game review
Coverage
Historical English reviews (2012–2023) for Counter-Strike (including CS:GO-era), PUBG and GTA V. Pilot of 300 reviews (100 per game), drawn from a 30,462-review English hash sample with both recommendation labels. Separate supplement of 71 gender/age candidates. Separate helpful-review subset for exploratory toxicity keyword analysis.
Access
Local CSV data accessed via Kaggle

Ethical considerations

  • User IDs omitted; public examples are paraphrased
  • Identity claims in reviews are unverified
  • Keyword matches are candidate flags, not confirmed categories or prevalence estimates
